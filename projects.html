Our work

Selected Projects

A selection of research and technology transfer projects reflecting our work in numerical modelling, machine learning and artificial intelligence.

DAMSHAI

2025–2028 · Dams

Dam Structural Health Monitoring and Safety Assessment with an AI Agent

Development and assessment of AI agents for dam safety, integrating monitoring data, numerical models and expert knowledge to support structural health monitoring and decision-making.

More information

PRONURB

2024–2027 · Environmental Engineering

Urban Air Quality Forecasting

Development of urban-scale forecasting tools for NO₂ and PM₂.₅ concentrations, combining numerical models and artificial intelligence techniques.

RESILINK

2024–2026 · Industrial Engineering

Resilient Buildings Equipped with Shear-Link Dissipators

Numerical modelling and optimization of structural energy dissipation systems based on shear-link devices, supporting the design of more resilient buildings.

DIGIT4WATER

2022–2025 · Environmental Engineering

Digital Tools for Advanced Wastewater Treatment

Development of machine learning models and digital decision-support tools to predict pollutant removal efficiency in advanced tertiary wastewater treatment processes.

More information

DOLMEN

2022–2026 · Dams

Dynamic Warning Thresholds for Dam Safety

Development of hybrid approaches combining finite element simulations and machine learning to predict dam behaviour and establish dynamic warning thresholds.

TRISTAN

2019–2022 · Dams

New Computational Tools for Reliability-Based Dam Safety Assessment

Development of computational methodologies for dam safety assessment incorporating reliability analysis, numerical simulations and data-driven techniques.

OPTIPRO

2020–2023 · Industrial Engineering

Intelligent Optimization of Rotary Metal Forming Processes

Advanced numerical simulation and machine learning techniques for the optimization of rotary metal forming processes and the development of digital twins.

More information

ACROPOLIS

2020–2022 · Dams

Reservoir Risk Classification Using GIS and Machine Learning

Development of machine learning surrogate models and GIS-based tools to assess potential consequences of off-stream reservoir failure.
